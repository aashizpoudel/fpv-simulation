Flight Tips & Gamepad

• Angle Mode: Self-leveling mode. Recommended for beginners (press F).

• Hover Throttle: Approx. 74% throttle needed to lift off at 3.8V; varies as battery discharges.

• Recording: Press G to start recording. Press G again to stop. Export from Settings.

• USB Radio / Gamepad: Plug in your radio or gamepad and calibrate in Settings for smooth stick response.
